01 About MESC

An engineering company built on trust

Founded in 1997 by Engineer Mohamed Anwar, MESC is an engineering company specializing in fire protection, pumping systems, plumbing, and HVAC applications.

Over nearly three decades, MESC has established itself as a trusted partner in the Egyptian market, combining engineering expertise, high-quality products, and dependable technical support to serve major commercial, industrial, and infrastructure projects.

Through strong industry partnerships and advanced engineering capabilities, MESC provides reliable systems and services that meet international standards while supporting the safety, efficiency, and long-term performance of every project it serves.

Who we are

A specialized engineering company

We are a specialized engineering company operating in the field of fire protection, pumping systems, plumbing, and HVAC solutions. We serve commercial, industrial, and infrastructure projects by combining technical expertise, globally recognized technologies, and strong engineering capabilities to deliver reliable and compliant systems.

What we do

From installation to after-sales

At MESC, we deliver comprehensive engineering services for fire protection and mechanical systems — including installation, testing, hydraulic calculations, commissioning, maintenance, and after-sales support. We also supply high-quality products from leading global manufacturers, ensuring reliable, efficient, and industry-compliant solutions.

Our vision

A stronger culture of safety

To advance a stronger culture of safety by promoting knowledge, awareness, and best practice in fire protection and mechanical systems — enabling organizations to make informed decisions that save lives and property, safeguard assets, and maximize long-term investment value.

Our mission

Performance that balances quality, efficiency & cost

To deliver reliable MEP products and engineering solutions supported by high service standards, technical expertise, and trusted global partnerships — providing high-performance systems that balance quality, efficiency, and whole-life cost.
